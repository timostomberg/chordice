Chordice Help

Help

Chordice generates chord progressions for practice.

View

  • Instrument tabs: Piano shows the notes of a clicked chord, Guitar its shape.
  • Roman numerals button: shows or hides the roman numerals.
  • Chord tones button: shows the notes of each chord. Greyed out on the guitar.
  • Colours button: colours the chords by their function: tonic, subdominant or dominant.
  • Painting button: shows or hides the watercolour behind the chords.
  • Dark mode button: switches the theme.

Rest the mouse on a button to see what it does.

The options panel

The Options button shows or hides it. Click a section's title to open or close it; an open section ends with a short note on what it does.

Progressions

Pick progressions by genre or one by one, e.g. the Pop Progression I – V – vi – IV, in C: C – G – Am – F. The number button sets how many follow each other: 1, 2, 4 or 8.

Progression variations

Rotations: how often a row starts on another chord. Permutations: how often its chords are shuffled. 0 means never, 5 always.

Chord variations

Pick extensions such as 7, maj7 or add9; Amount sets how often a chord takes one; at 5+ every chord takes one, and the plain chord only stays where no picked extension fits. With #b off, extensions with tones foreign to the key are left out; unselect all switches every extension off.

Bass variations

Amount: how often a chord gets another bass note, e.g. C/E. Greyed out on the guitar.

Melody variations

The Melody button shows each chord in an inversion chosen by its top note. Strength: how far the top notes may jump. Greyed out on the guitar.

Exercises

The Exercises button shows or hides them. An exercise fixes some options for one kind of practice; they are greyed out meanwhile. unselect all switches it off. Circle around ii7-V7-Imaj7 plays the Jazz Cadence in every key, with only 7 and maj7.

Saved songs

The Songs button shows or hides them. Name the song if you like and press the floppy disk. A click on a saved song brings it back; the pencil renames it, the cross deletes it after asking. At most 75 songs can be saved.

If Chordice has changed since, a song is shown as it was saved, with its options fixed, until Generate anew or New Song.

Account

The Account button shows or hides the account. Sign in with Google keeps your songs and settings in your account, on every device. Sign out leaves them in your account and removes the songs from this browser; Delete account deletes everything, after Google has asked you to sign in once more.

Your settings are kept in this browser and, signed in, in your account. Safari forgets them after seven days without a visit, unless Chordice is on the home screen.

Reset settings sets every setting back to its default. Your saved songs stay.

The circle of fifths

The circle of fifths left of the chords chooses the key; its relative minor is shown inside, e.g. C and Am.

The arrow button in the middle of the circle gives one row per key, counterclockwise from C (C, F, B♭, …). The circle is greyed out meanwhile.

Viewing the chords

With the Chord tones button on, the notes of each chord are shown beside it, and with the Melody button on, its melody note too.

  • Blue tones: added by an extension. Red tones: foreign to the key.
  • Square brackets: the melody note.

Click a chord to open its notes. Click its left or right half, swipe or use the arrow keys for the previous or next inversion or shape. Escape closes it. The instrument tabs choose what it shows:

  • Piano: the notes on a staff.
  • Guitar: a diagram in standard tuning, or of the most similar chord.

New Song

The New Song button (bottom right) gives new rows with the current settings.

On a phone or tablet

  • Home screen: in Safari, Share, then Add to Home Screen: Chordice opens as an app.
  • Without a network: once opened online, Chordice also works offline.
  • Screen: the screen stays on while Chordice is shown.
  • Zoom: pinch the chords.
