Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains which personal data Chordice processes, why, and what rights you have. Chordice shows no ads, uses no analytics and does not track you.

Hosting

Chordice is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the page, your browser sends GitHub technical data such as your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser's user agent. GitHub uses it to deliver the page and to keep its service secure, and may keep it in log files for a short time. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the page safely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Libraries from content delivery networks

Chordice loads some program libraries from content delivery networks: the music libraries and the guitar chord shapes from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), and, only if you use the account, the Firebase libraries from Google (www.gstatic.com). Your browser then sends your IP address and technical data such as the user agent to these networks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the page quickly and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Once loaded, the libraries are stored in your browser, so they are not loaded again on every visit. More: jsDelivr privacy policy, Google Privacy Policy.

Storage in your browser

Chordice keeps what it needs to work in your browser:

  • Your settings in a cookie (chordice_state, kept for one year) and in a copy in the local storage.
  • Your saved songs in the local storage (chordice_songs), while you are not signed in.
  • The page and its libraries in the cache of a service worker, so that Chordice also works without a network.

This data stays on your device and is not evaluated by us; the cookie is sent along when the page is loaded, as browsers do with every cookie, but it is not used there. Storing it is strictly necessary for the service you ask for (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is needed. You can delete it at any time in the settings of your browser; Chordice then starts with its default settings and without saved songs.

Account (optional)

You can use Chordice without an account. If you sign in with Google, your saved songs are kept in your account, so that you find them on every device. For this we use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

We process:

  • the data of your Google account that Google passes on when you sign in: your name, your email address, the address of your profile picture and your Google user ID;
  • an ID of your account, and the times of creating it and of signing in;
  • your saved songs (their names, when they were saved, the options they were made with and their chords);
  • technical data that arises when your browser connects to Google, such as your IP address.

We use this data only to provide the account and to keep your songs. The legal basis is the performance of the contract of use that you enter into by signing in (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The songs are stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). The account data of Firebase Authentication may also be processed by Google in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and standard contractual clauses apply too. While you are signed in, a copy of your songs is also kept in your browser, so that they are there without a network; signing out removes it from the browser.

Your data is kept as long as you have the account. Delete account in the Songs panel deletes your account and every song in it at once. Signing out keeps them in your account. More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable form (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21). To exercise them, write to the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live.

Other

You are not obliged to provide any personal data; without an account, Chordice works in full, except that your songs are kept only in your browser. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.
